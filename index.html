Guillaume Loignon

Professor, Department of Education and Pedagogy
Université du Québec à Montréal (UQAM)
Montréal, Québec, Canada

loignon.guillaume@uqam.ca

Research Interests

  • Text complexity and readability
  • Natural language processing and computational linguistics
  • Educational assessment and measurement
  • Psycholinguistics and philosophy of language
  • Automated essay scoring

Software

ALSI / ILSA — Analyseur Lexico-Syntaxique Intégré (Integrated Lexical-Syntactic Analyzer). A French linguistic complexity analyzer computing approximately 180 lexical and syntactic features from text. Written in R. Freely available on GitHub.
